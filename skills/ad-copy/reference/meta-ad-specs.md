# Meta Ad Specs and Policy Rules (for copy)

Working rules the skill checks every ad against. Meta changes limits now and then. If a number here looks off, verify at https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide before publishing.

## Character limits (what actually shows on mobile)

| Field | Shows on mobile before cutoff | Hard limit | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary text | About 125 characters, then "...See more" | No hard limit (keep long copy under ~1,500 chars) | The first 125 characters must contain the full opener hook. Never let the cutoff land mid-idea. |
| Headline | About 27 characters on feed, 40 on some placements | 255 | Outcome first. Put "without [objection]" after a pipe if room. |
| Description | About 27 characters (often hidden on mobile feed) | 255 | Treat as optional. Never rely on it for a key message. |
| Link display | Domain only | | Clean domain, no long path. |

## Where each piece shows

| Placement | Primary text | Headline | Image text |
|---|---|---|---|
| Facebook feed | Yes, above image | Yes, below image | Yes |
| Instagram feed | Yes, below image, 2 lines then "more" | Not shown | Yes (this is the only headline the reader sees) |
| Stories / Reels | 1 to 2 lines at the bottom, often covered by the CTA | Not shown | Yes (carries everything) |

Rule that falls out of this: **the opener and the on-image text must each work alone.** On Instagram, the reader may never see the link headline.

## Copy structure for one ad

1. **Opener** (first line, under 125 characters). Stops the scroll. This is the "Alternative Headline."
2. **Body** (build intent). Uses the ingredients list from the 101 doc.
3. **CTA line** (tell them exactly what to click and what happens next).
4. **Headline** (outcome, passes the Classified Ad Test).
5. **Description** (optional template line).
6. **On-image headline** (3 to 8 words, hands off to the image skill).

## Meta policy rules that get ads rejected or accounts flagged

Write around these. Do not just soften them. Rewrite the idea.

- **No personal attributes.** Never say or imply the reader has a trait: "Are you overweight?", "Struggling with depression?", "For people with bad credit." Instead speak about the group in third person or the topic: "Why do busy moms struggle to lose weight?" not "Are you a busy mom struggling to lose weight?"
- **No before/after body images or copy that implies unrealistic results.** "Lose 50 lbs in 2 months" is a rejection risk. Use ranges, testimonials with disclaimers, or process-focused outcomes.
- **No guaranteed income or earnings claims.** "Make $10k/month" gets flagged. "How James built a $10k/month store" as a case study with context is safer. Add "results not typical" where needed.
- **No health cure claims.** "Cures," "treats," "eliminates" for medical conditions is a rejection. "Relieve," "support," "reduce symptoms" is safer.
- **No sensational language or excessive punctuation.** No "!!!" or ALL CAPS whole sentences. Single-word caps for emphasis is fine.
- **No engagement bait.** "Comment YES if you agree," "Tag a friend" in ad copy gets ads throttled.
- **No misleading urgency.** "Only 3 spots left" must be true.
- **Special categories.** Housing, credit, employment, and social/political issues have restricted targeting and stricter copy rules. Flag these to the user.

## Reading level and style

- Grade 5 to 7 reading level. Short sentences. One idea per line.
- One blank line between every sentence or two on mobile. Walls of text die.
- No em dashes. Use periods, commas, or line breaks.
- Emojis: 0 to 3 per ad, used as bullets or a single accent, never in the opener.
- Numbers as digits, odd numbers beat round ones ("27 days" beats "a month").

## Objective changes the CTA

| Objective | CTA line pattern | Button |
|---|---|---|
| Lead magnet / free training | "Click the link, and on the next page I'll show you [thing]." | Learn more / Sign up |
| Book a call | "Click below, answer 3 quick questions, and pick a time that works." | Book now / Apply now |
| Buy now (low ticket) | "Click, grab it for [price], and start [outcome] tonight." | Shop now / Get offer |
| DM / Messenger | "Tap Send message and say [word]. I'll reply personally." | Send message |
| Retargeting | Shorter copy, direct reminder, proof, objection handle, one CTA. | Matches original |

## Traffic temperature changes the opener

- **Cold:** Lead with problem, misconception, or "why" question. Never lead with the offer.
- **Warm (engaged, watched video, visited site):** Lead with mechanism or proof. Offer can show up earlier.
- **Hot (cart abandon, page visit, lead not converted):** Lead with the objection or the deadline. Short.
